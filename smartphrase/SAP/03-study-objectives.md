# 3. Study Objectives

## 3.1. Primary Objective

To assess progression-free survival in post-menopausal patients with hormone-receptor
positive metastatic breast cancer that is resistant to aromatase inhibitor (AI) therapy treated
with fulvestrant and everolimus compared to fulvestrant alone.

## 3.2. Secondary Objectives

To describe the safety profile, objective response rate, time to progression and overall survival
in post-menopausal patients with hormone-receptor positive metastatic breast cancer that is
resistant to aromatase inhibitor (AI) therapy treated with fulvestrant and everolimus compared
to fulvestrant alone.
